  1. 2026 pitch-err a wearable that notices filler words while you speak and answers with a small vibration.

    a wearable system that detects filler words while someone is speaking and responds with a small vibration. audio is recorded by a raspberry pi pico, processed locally on a raspberry pi, and turned into haptic feedback in real time.

    the project covers the pico firmware, dataset preparation, model training, evaluation, and live inference on a raspberry pi 5.

    source

  2. 2026 mailern an iphone mail app where the goal is to keep your inbox as clean as possible

    i built mailern around the inbox zero philosophy.

    incoming items go to the inbox; if i can’t deal with it yet, i snooze it. if i don't think it needs my attention, i settle it. if i reply to it, it gets settled automatically until it gets a new reply, which brings it back to the inbox.

    it is a native swiftui app that connects to a go service over tailscale, which handles all the gmail, icloud mail, and proton mail integrations.

    source
