i study computer science and speech technology, and i spend a lot of time making things with software.

  1. 2026 pitch-err a wearable that notices filler words while you speak and answers with a small vibration.

    a wearable system that detects filler words while someone is speaking and responds with a small vibration. audio is recorded by a raspberry pi pico, processed locally on a raspberry pi, and turned into haptic feedback in real time.

    the project covers the pico firmware, dataset preparation, model training, evaluation, and live inference on a raspberry pi 5.

    wearable · python, raspberry pisource

  2. 2026 dashboard one private place for my calendar, email, pull requests, notes, and codex tasks.

    a private dashboard i built around the information and tools i use every day. it collects my calendar, email, github pull requests, notes, and codex tasks in one place.

    it also creates a daily brief and can suggest work to hand off to codex. every suggestion has to be reviewed before anything starts, and factual claims link back to their source.

    personal tool · typescriptsource

  3. 2025 index8 bookmarks you find again by meaning instead of exact words.

    a bookmarking tool for saving links and text and finding them again by meaning rather than exact words.

    index8 grew into a full application with a web interface, backend, encrypted storage, semantic search, an http api, a command-line client, and a raycast extension.

    app · typescriptvisit

  4. now synapse conversations on an infinite canvas, where messages and ai replies branch and reconnect.

    a very early project i’m working on with my friend levi kingma. synapse puts conversations on an infinite canvas, where messages, files, and ai responses can branch, reconnect, and refer to one another.

    we work on it on and off, and we are still figuring out what it should become.

    canvas · with levi kingmavisit
