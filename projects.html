a selection of things i’ve made. some started as school or research projects, while others exist because i wanted something that did not exist yet.

pitch-err

a wearable system that detects filler words while someone is speaking and responds with a small vibration. audio is recorded by a raspberry pi pico, processed locally on a raspberry pi, and turned into haptic feedback in real time.

the project includes the hardware, firmware, dataset preparation, model training, evaluation, and live inference software.

  • index8

    a bookmarking tool for saving links and text and finding them again by meaning rather than exact words.

    index8 grew into a full application with a web interface, backend, encrypted storage, semantic search, an http api, a command-line client, and a raycast extension.
